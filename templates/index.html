What is Second Serve?

Second Serve is a smart food rescue and redistribution system designed to reduce food wastage by connecting food donors with verified NGOs. Instead of allowing surplus food to go to waste, the platform helps redirect it to people who need it.

How It Works

1. Donate Food

Donors can provide information about surplus food available for donation.

2. NGO Receives Request

Registered NGOs can view available food donations and respond to suitable requests.

3. Food Reaches People

The donated food is collected and distributed to people in need through the NGO.
